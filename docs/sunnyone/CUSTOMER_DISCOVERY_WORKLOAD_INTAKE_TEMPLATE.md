## A. Problem and current workflow

1. What business process are you considering using generative AI for?
2. What happens today without AI?
3. Which exact tasks are automated or assisted?
4. What is the current provider, model or hardware (if known)?
5. What is the actual monthly spend, invoice range or internal cost? May we record a figure or anonymised range?
6. What is not working well: cost, latency, quality, privacy, reliability, language support, integration or something else?
7. What measurable business outcome would make a replacement worthwhile?

## D. Commercial fit

1. Who owns the budget and signs the contract?
2. How is the current solution paid for: per token, per request, per seat, reserved capacity, GPU-hour or fixed contract?
3. What monthly budget has been approved or spent? Distinguish actual spend from a hypothetical budget.
4. What pricing unit would be easiest to procure?
5. What measurable saving or performance improvement is needed to consider switching?
6. What migration, security review and procurement steps are required?
7. Would the organisation consider a controlled paid pilot? What would the pilot need to prove?
8. What is the expected decision timeline?

Do not quote a SUNNYONE price until measured costs and commercial assumptions are available. Do not promise a capability that has not been demonstrated.

Score interpretation:
- 0 = unknown / unsupported
- 1 = partial / proxy evidence
- 2 = direct and documented evidence

A high score is a prioritisation aid only. It does not establish a contract or guarantee profitability.

## G. Workload trace privacy and safety

Collect only data the customer is authorised to share. Prefer synthetic or redacted prompts. Do not request secrets, personal data or confidential source documents unless a lawful, secure processing arrangement is established. Store dataset provenance, permission, redaction and retention rules. Never publish customer prompts or identifiers in the public repository.
